BASIC MAINTENANCE TIPS

BATTERY
Check the battery terminals for corrosion and ensure the battery is securely fastened.
LIGHTS
Verify that all headlights, taillights, brake lights, and turn signals are functioning correctly.
OIL
Check the engine oil level and ensure it's within the recommended range.
WATER
Inspect the coolant level and check for any leaks in the cooling system.
BRAKES
Ensure the brakes are in good working order and check the brake fluid level.
AIR
Check the tire pressure and ensure it's within the recommended range.
GAS
Make sure you have enough fuel for your trip.
ENGINE
Listen for any unusual noises or check for any leaks or damage.
TIRES
Check the tire tread depth and ensure the tires are properly inflated.
SELF
Ensure you are in good physical and mental condition to drive safely.
